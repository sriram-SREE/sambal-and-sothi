Our Heritage

Every meal is a celebration

Heritage, flavors and togetherness — served from one kitchen on Chander Road, every day of the year.

At Sambal & Sothi, every meal is a celebration of heritage, flavors, and togetherness. Inspired by the rich culinary traditions of South India and Sri Lanka, we bring generations of authentic recipes to the table, prepared with fresh ingredients and time-honored techniques.

From our signature creamy Sothi and fiery Sambal to fragrant biryanis, handcrafted thosai, crispy vadai, and comforting curries, every dish is made to deliver bold, memorable flavors that feel like home.

Whether you're gathering with family, catching up with friends, or simply craving a comforting meal, Sambal & Sothi offers a warm and welcoming dining experience where tradition meets quality.

Authentic Flavors. Timeless Traditions. Unforgettable Experiences.
